I am a software engineer and entrepreneur focused on cloud platforms, developer experience, and data-driven products. I enjoy turning complex technical problems into practical, maintainable solutions.

## Work

My experience spans software engineering, cloud and DevOps consulting, and technical leadership. I have designed cloud-native platforms, built delivery automation and internal developer tooling, and helped teams improve how they develop and operate software.

At Microsoft, I worked with engineering teams on application modernization and platform projects. I also contributed to [EVE](https://www.starteve.ai/), an accessibility product that creates live captions and translations, gaining hands-on experience building and evolving software in a small, fast-moving team.

## Ventures

I co-founded [mint software](https://mmmint.ai), a technology company centered on applied engineering and data-driven products. I am also a co-founder of mint future, the company behind [wirkaufendeinethg.de](https://www.wirkaufendeinethg.de/), a digital platform for services around electric mobility and emissions trading.

These ventures have given me a broader perspective on product development, sustainable business models, and the realities of building technology companies.

## Community

I enjoy sharing what I learn through mentoring, teaching, open-source contributions, and [technical talks](/talks.html). I have volunteered with [ReDI School](https://www.redi-school.org/), taught introductory programming to newcomers pursuing careers in technology, and helped host the [PowerShell User Group Munich](https://github.com/GPSUG).

I also contributed to the [DevOps Dojo](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/devops-dojo-white-belt-foundation/), a public Microsoft Learn path for building multidisciplinary DevOps teams through shared practices, processes, and tools.
